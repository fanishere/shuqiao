# Shuqiao

A Chinese learning app for HSK 2–6+ learners to learn and review vocabulary and grammar.

Vocab and grammar are exercised only through written and spoken **composition**, not flash cards — you prove you know a concept by using it. Concepts you've learned are tracked to drive a spaced-repetition review schedule.

## Assumptions

- One learner (the developer) for now.
- Simplified characters.

## Principles

These shape every feature; check designs against them.

1. **Production is the test.** Recognizing a word is not knowing it. An item graduates only when used correctly, unprompted, in more than one context.
2. **Small focus, woven everywhere.** Each week has 5–8 focus items. Every prompt, drill and conversation invites them, so you meet them many times in different shapes.
3. **Your mistakes are curriculum.** Recurring errors (的/得/地, 了 placement, 认为 vs 以为) become tracked items with their own review, just like vocab.
4. **The learner drives, the syllabus is a map.** HSK lists place you and suggest gaps, but what you write, read, and get wrong sets the agenda.
5. **Attempt before answer.** You always try first. Model answers and native rewrites appear after your attempt and revision, never before.
6. **Feedback you can absorb.** A capped number of comments, ranked by importance, beats a page of red ink.
7. **The memory is visible.** You can see what the app thinks you know, why, and correct it.
